Grief is one of the most profound experiences we go through, and everyone experiences it differently. Whether your loss is recent or something you have been carrying for a long time, counselling can offer a space to express what you are feeling without judgement, and to find your own way through.

Loss comes in many forms — the death of someone close, the end of a relationship, a change in health, or the loss of a sense of who you are. All of these deserve to be taken seriously and given space.

I will not rush you or tell you how grief should look. I will simply be here to listen and to walk alongside you as you find your way forward.
