If you’re reading this, then I’m guessing that, put simply, you would like to believe in and trust yourself more. 

You’re probably either exhausted by pretending you’ve got everything in hand when inside you’re wracked with self doubt, or frustrated that you’ve talked yourself out of yet another opportunity because you don’t think you can do it. Perhaps you’re overwhelmed by the constant replay of conversations – Did I upset that person? Did I talk about myself too much? Why do I feel guilty when I put myself first?

These are all ways in which low self esteem or confidence can show up.

Clients often talk about the ‘knowing’ cognitively that they are essentially a worthy, capable person, with the right to make mistakes and worthy of love and respect. However, they also wrestle with a niggling feeling that deep down that perhaps they are not. They harbour a deep seated fear that they are in fact somehow not ok as they are, and that one day, people will find out.

If this is you, then therapy can help put you back in touch with self belief, enabling you to feel more solid and at ease with yourself.

You may have talked yourself out of accessing therapy numerous times, perhaps worried about taking up space or perhaps even the thought of even talking about yourself fills you with guilt or excruciating discomfort. Or you may be really ready to start.

Either way I would really welcome you to get in touch. We can have an informal chat about what’s going on, as well as a gentle exploration of the ways in which counselling can help you access your confidence again.
