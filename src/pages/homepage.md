I am an experienced psychotherapeutic counsellor and support group facilitator, based in Hove and online.

With over 10 years experience, I have witnessed time and again the transformative power of being listened to by someone who is trained to hear and respond in ways that help facilitate positive change.

I am passionate about supporting people with their mental health and wellbeing - helping people feel more at ease in their own skin, and empowered enough to make changes in their life.

I am friendly, approachable and down to earth, so please reach out to ask any questions. I'd love to hear from you.
