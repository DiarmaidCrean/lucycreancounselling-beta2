## Fees:

**Introduction session: £20**\
This is an in person, 50 minute session, in which we will explore what's bringing you to therapy and how we can work on that in our sessions. Essentially it is an opportunity to ensure we are a 'good fit', with no obligation to book again if it doesn't feel right.

I can also offer a free 20 minute telephone call to discuss whether what you hope to work on is something I can help with.

**50 minute session: £55-£65 (based on affordability)**\
I passionately believe therapy should be accessible, and therefore I offer a sliding scale based on what you can afford.

## How to book

If you are interested in booking a session, please get in touch using the form below, or via email or phone. We can arrange either a free 20 minute phone call, or a reduced rate introduction session.
