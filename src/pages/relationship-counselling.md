If you find yourself struggling in a relationship, whether this is a partner, an ex-partner, friend or family member, therapy can be a fantastic resource to consider. 

I often hear clients describe dynamics in a relationship that leave them feeling lost, confused or drained by a particular relationship. You may find it hard to find your voice with this person. Or perhaps you can see patterns – certain cyclical arguments that never seem to progress and leave you feeling exhausted or angry.

“I don’t know if I’m asking for too much or too little”

“How can I say no without feeling guilty, or yes without feeling resentful?”

“I feel angry and I don’t understand why”

If any of this sounds familiar to you, then counselling can really help you get to the heart of the issue. Some space away from the relationship to unpack what has been going on and freely express yourself.

Whilst I do not offer couple or family therapy, individual therapy can be a helpful way to explore what is going on in a relationship dynamic, as well as considering what you need and ways to implement positive shifts. 

If you are struggling with a relationship, then please reach out for an introduction session or call.
